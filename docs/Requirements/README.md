# Design opportunity and experience requirements

- [Design opportunity](Design_Opportunity.md): research-based opportunity and intended gallery experience.
- [Experience requirements](Experience_Requirements.md): ER1–ER6, their research basis and current review after Iterations 2 and 3.
- [Research evidence](../Research/README.md): interviews, literature and survey evidence.
- [Evaluation evidence](../Evaluation/README.md): testing findings and remaining questions.

The original requirements concern independent interpretation, attention to physical artworks, optional participation, different perspectives, brief digital interaction and control over sharing. The current review records partial support and unresolved tensions; implemented features do not automatically satisfy or validate every requirement.

See the published [Design Process Overview](https://github.com/iris0324/babear_deco3500/wiki/Design-Process-Overview) for design changes and [Ethical Considerations](https://github.com/iris0324/babear_deco3500/wiki/Ethical-Considerations) for privacy, participation and safety trade-offs.
