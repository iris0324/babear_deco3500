# Borrowed Eyes — Survey Results

## 1. Survey Overview

This exploratory questionnaire collected responses from **30 respondents** to understand preferences, expectations, and concerns about participating in social artwork-based activities in galleries.

The questionnaire contained **23 questions** across four parts:

- **Part A — Gallery Visiting Preferences (Q1–Q4)**
- **Part B — Blind Eyes (Q5–Q11)**
- **Part C — Together / Face-to-Face (Q12–Q19)**
- **Part D — Group Formation (Q20–Q23)**

Questions included single-choice items, multiple-choice items, 1–5 Likert-scale statements, and an open-ended question. The PDF version of the questionnaire documents the **questions and response options**, not the full response distribution. This report therefore presents only the numerical results currently available from the team's 30-response findings summary. It is a **partial descriptive results report**, not a full question-by-question export.

**Reporting convention:** Percentages are rounded to the nearest whole number. Counts use **n = 30** as the denominator. For multiple-choice questions, percentages need not sum to 100%.

## 2. Available Survey Results

### Part A — Gallery Visiting Preferences

| Question / measure | Response reported | Count (n=30) | Percentage |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Q2: What do you usually enjoy doing when visiting an art gallery? | Looking at artworks quietly | 21 | 70% |
| Q3: I feel confident sharing my interpretation of an artwork with other visitors. | Respondents reported as confident in the team's findings summary | 9 | 30% |

**Data note:** Q2 allowed multiple selections. The 9/30 figure for Q3 is reproduced from the existing team summary; the precise threshold used to classify respondents as “confident” (for example, Likert responses 4–5) should be confirmed against the Forms response export.

### Part B — Blind Eyes

| Question / measure | Response reported | Count (n=30) | Percentage |

### Part C — Together / Face-to-Face

| Question / measure | Response reported | Count (n=30) | Percentage |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Q12: Comfortable joining a group of unfamiliar visitors | Respondents reported as comfortable | 8 | 27% |
| Q16: What would encourage participation in Together? | Having a clear goal | 15 | 50% |
| Q18: Concerns about Together | One person might dominate the discussion | 14 | 47% |

**Open-ended question Q19:** The team's summary identifies recurring ideas of **shared goals**, **building on one another's observations**, and **being able to contribute without having to speak extensively**. The original answers and coding counts were not supplied, so no frequency is assigned to these themes.

**Data note:** Q16 and Q18 allowed multiple selections. For Q12, the reported comfort classification needs confirmation from the response export.

## 5. Methodological Notes and Limitations

- This is a **small exploratory questionnaire** (n = 30). The available material does not establish a representative sampling method or respondent demographics.
- Survey results report **self-described preferences, intentions, and concerns**, not observed social behaviour.
- The figures in this document were obtained from the team's existing findings summary; they have **not been independently recalculated from raw Google Forms responses**.
- For Likert-scale questions, confirmation of the exact categories grouped as agreement/comfort/confidence is still needed.
- Multiple-choice response categories can overlap; counts should not be interpreted as mutually exclusive unless the questionnaire specifies one response only.
- The survey PDF is the **questionnaire instrument**. It does not contain answer charts or a response-level dataset.
- Results from prototype testing must be reported separately, because those sessions measured interactions rather than questionnaire choices.
