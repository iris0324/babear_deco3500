# Design Opportunity

## Opportunity Statement

There is an opportunity to use artworks as shared conversation anchors, helping gallery visitors move beyond discussing art to sharing personal experiences, understanding different backgrounds, and building meaningful human connections.

## Research-Informed Opportunity

Research on museum experiences, visitor participation, and social interaction suggests that artworks can provide shared starting points for communication and perspective exchange.

Our exploratory survey of 30 respondents showed that 70% preferred quiet art viewing, while only 30% felt confident sharing their interpretations with other visitors. These findings highlight the importance of providing different levels of social participation.

Our prototype testing also showed opportunities for perspective exchange in Blind Eyes and collaborative decision-making in Face-to-Face. However, completing artwork-finding tasks did not necessarily lead to deeper mutual understanding.

Together, these findings informed our opportunity to explore how artwork-based interactions might encourage personal expression, perspective exchange, and meaningful human connections.

## Key Design Opportunities

**1. Artworks as Conversation Anchors**

Use artworks as shared starting points that encourage visitors to initiate conversations and exchange ideas.

**2. Personal Expression and Perspective Exchange**

Encourage visitors to share their feelings, personal experiences, and interpretations while exploring how others see the same artwork differently.

**3. Mutual Understanding**

Create opportunities for visitors to listen, respond, and explore the experiences and perspectives behind different interpretations.

**4. Flexible Social Participation**

Provide different levels of social interaction, allowing visitors to participate according to their preferences and social comfort.

## Design Goal

To explore how artworks can support personal expression, perspective exchange, and mutual understanding, creating opportunities for meaningful human connections while respecting visitors' different social preferences.
