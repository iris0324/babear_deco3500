# Design

This folder documents the development and iteration of our design concepts.

Our design process uses findings from research and experience requirements to explore how social and contextual computing can support gallery visitors.

The design has developed through multiple iterations:

> **Research → Design Concept → Prototype → Evaluation → Design Iteration**

---

## Design Iteration 1 — Art Persona

Our first concept, **Art Persona**, used playful prompts to encourage visitors to observe artworks and form their own interpretations.

The concept explored:

- personal interpretation;
- playful artwork observation;
- reducing the pressure of finding a "correct" meaning;
- anonymous sharing of visitor perspectives.

Early evaluation showed that the concept supported individual reflection, but the social interaction between visitors was limited.

This led us to explore how one visitor's contribution could more directly shape another visitor's gallery experience.

See: [Design Iteration 1](Design_Iteration_1.md)

---

## Design Iteration 2 — Borrowed Eyes

**Borrowed Eyes** develops the social interaction further by allowing visitors to leave a way of looking at an artwork for another visitor.

The experience uses:

- a **Visual Fragment** from an artwork;
- a **Clue** to support exploration;
- a **Visitor Perspective** revealed after independent interpretation;
- a **Contribution** created for a future visitor.

The core interaction is:

> **Choose → Find → Interpret → Compare → Re-observe → Contribute**

This creates an asynchronous visitor-to-visitor experience without requiring direct conversation between strangers.
