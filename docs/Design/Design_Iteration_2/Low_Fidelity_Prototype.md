# Borrowed Eyes — Low-Fidelity Paper Prototype

## 1. Prototype Overview

Borrowed Eyes is a cumulative, visitor-generated gallery experience.

The low-fidelity prototype explores how one visitor's way of looking can become part of another visitor's gallery experience.

The prototype focuses on three types of visitor-generated content:

- **Visual Fragment** — a cropped detail from an artwork.
- **Clue** — a short hint that helps another visitor find the artwork.
- **Perspective** — a visitor's personal interpretation of the selected detail.

The visual fragment and clue are available before the search.

The previous visitor's perspective remains hidden until the current visitor has formed their own interpretation.

---

## 2. Prototype Components

### Visual Fragment Card

Each card shows a cropped visual detail from an artwork.

The full artwork, title, artist name, and exact location are not shown.

The fragment is designed to create curiosity and encourage visitors to explore the physical gallery.

### Progressive Clues

Each card includes:

- **Clue 1** — a short first hint;
- **Clue 2** — an optional second hint if the visitor is stuck.

The clues support exploration without directly revealing the artwork.

### Participant Response

After finding the artwork, the visitor first records:

- what they noticed;
- what the selected detail makes them think or feel.

Only after this independent response is completed is the previous visitor's perspective revealed.

The visitor can then compare the two interpretations.

### Leave Your Eyes

At the end of the experience, the visitor selects a different artwork and creates:

> **Visual Fragment + Clue + Personal Perspective**

This content can then become part of a future visitor's experience.

---

## 3. Initial Cards

Before the main testing sessions, four initial contributors create the starting content for Borrowed Eyes.

Each contributor provides:

- one selected artwork;
- one visual detail;
- one short clue;
- one personal interpretation.

These contributions are prepared as **four initial visitor-created cards**.

The initial cards allow the prototype to begin with real visitor-generated perspectives rather than content created only by the design team.

See the [`initial_cards`](initial_cards/) folder for the four starting cards.

---

## 4. Core Interaction

The prototype follows six stages:

### 1. Choose

The visitor chooses a visual fragment that makes them curious.

### 2. Find

The visitor uses the clue to search for the related artwork.

### 3. Interpret

After finding the artwork, the visitor forms their own interpretation.

### 4. Compare

The previous visitor's perspective is revealed.

The visitor compares it with their own interpretation.

### 5. Re-observe

The visitor looks at the physical artwork again and considers whether the other perspective changes what they notice.

### 6. Contribute

The visitor creates a new visual fragment, clue, and perspective for a future visitor.

The overall interaction is:

> **Choose → Find → Interpret → Compare → Re-observe → Contribute**

---

## 5. Cumulative Social Interaction

Borrowed Eyes does not require visitors to meet or talk directly.

Instead, visitor perspectives are passed through the artwork:

> **Previous Visitor**
>  
> leaves a visual fragment, clue and perspective
>
> ↓
>
> **Current Visitor**
>
> explores, interprets and compares
>
> ↓
>
> leaves a new contribution
>
> ↓
>
> **Future Visitor**

This creates an asynchronous and cumulative visitor-to-visitor experience.

The interaction can be summarised as:

> **Borrow → Interpret → Leave**
