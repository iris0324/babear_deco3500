Created 18 October 2026 with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool. These are fictional decorative illustrations, not imported collection artworks or real visitor photographs. No gameplay answer image was used as a reference. The five previously approved homepage illustrations were preserved.

## Prompt specifications

Shared art direction: finished vintage museum oil painting; ivory parchment, deep forest green, walnut and antique brass; warm restrained lighting, detailed painterly material texture, elegant exhibition-catalogue composition. No lettering, logos, interface, modern objects, game answers or identifiable collection paintings. Horizontal scenes must remain legible in compact card crops. Portraits must be distinct fictional people in consistent classical oval brass frames, with clear faces and quiet green/ivory backgrounds.

## Usage and identity

Social records own stable simulated user IDs and portrait paths. Local players retain an intentional initial monogram, since no personal portrait was supplied. Existing custom/mock IDs receive a deterministic fallback from this five-portrait set; those fallback portraits are reused and do not claim a unique real identity.

Letter, study, salon and listening motifs identify feature categories. Reuse within the same category is intentional; they do not depict the selected challenge. The approved homepage imagery, imported artwork files, challenge JSON and manifest were not edited by this repair.
